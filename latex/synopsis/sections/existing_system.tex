\section{Existing System}
Conventional VPN solutions such as WireGuard, OpenVPN and IPsec primarily use
ECC- or RSA-based authentication and key exchange. These systems provide strong
security against classical attackers, but their public-key layer is vulnerable
to quantum attacks based on Shor's algorithm. Existing deployments also treat
post-quantum protection as an add-on rather than an integrated design goal,
which creates uncertainty in long-term confidentiality, downgrade resistance
and algorithm agility during future migration.
